summary:
  - In my time engineering, I've seen many opportunities regarding automation and potential in saving hours of Engineering effort. 
  - During my time in Redbubble, I've was able to spot such an opportunity. Often, artwork that voided the ToS of the webiste would appear, and need to be removed.
  - Cache invalidating these images was originally a manual effort, and required oversight from the Engineer that it was asked from to ensure the artwork was completely removed from the site.
  - The existing process itself was a repetitive, tedious effort, signaling that there was opportunity to shift effort from the Engineer to an engineered workflow.
  - An API being available provided a solution to the manual effort. Writing a supporting script for an Engineer that iterates over the URL's for the cached images and retries on errors would provide the outcome that would allow the team to focus more on the prioritised work rather than the BAU work.
  - From this effort, Engineers were able to invest more focus onto their work rather than being interrupted constantly by the repetitive work needed to deliver the cache. This was approximately 3-6 hours of work per week, meaning over a quarter on average 41 hours of effort was moved from BAU work to prioritised deliverables.
