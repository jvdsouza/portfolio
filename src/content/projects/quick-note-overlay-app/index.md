  - A quick application created through AI assisted development, this product's aim was to reduce overhead when needing a place to write a thought, idea, or breakthrough before it disappears.
  - This products initial mission was to manage anxiety developed by overwhelming thoughts passing through the mind through the means of quickly allowing the user to "braindump". 
  - It also aims to be very easy and reliable, being able to be reached at any time, very quickly, and in a single location.
  - Primarily for myself as a means to help with ADHD. I hope it'll  find its way to others, helping them with reducing the barriers when recording their fast paced thoughts.
